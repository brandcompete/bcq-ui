The <code>dropdown</code> component is used for collecting user provided information from a list of options, 
allowing single or multiple selection, filtering and much more. Works great with our <a href="/forms/async-form" v-link>async-form</a> 
and internally makes use of the awesome <a rel="noopener" href="https://github.com/Akryum/vue-virtual-scroller/blob/master/packages/vue-virtual-scroller/README.md" target="_blank">vue-virtual-scroller</a>,
developed by the founder of Vue <a rel="noopener" href="https://github.com/Akryum" target="_blank">Guillaume Chau</a>.
