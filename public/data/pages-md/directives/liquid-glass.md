A Vue <a href="/directives" v-link>directive</a> that applies Apple-style liquid glass to any element by rendering a translucent, frosted 
surface with dynamic blur and subtle light refraction. When attached, it creates a depth-aware effect 
that responds to background movement, giving the illusion of fluid glass floating above the UI. 
The directive automatically handles backdrop filtering, soft highlights, and edge diffusion for a polished look.
It is lightweight, customizable, and works seamlessly across components with minimal setup.
