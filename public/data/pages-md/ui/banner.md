A banner <a href="/ui" v-link>component</a> is a prominent UI element, similar to <a href="/ui/alert" v-link>alert</a> 
and <a href="/ui/empty" v-link>empty</a> used to display important messages, announcements, or status updates at the 
top or within a section of an interface. It helps draw the user's attention to key information such as alerts, promotions,
or guidance while maintaining visual consistency with the overall design.
