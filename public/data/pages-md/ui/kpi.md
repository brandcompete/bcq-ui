Our KPI <a href="/ui" v-link>component</a> is a flexible UI element designed to display key performance 
indicators with rich visual context. It supports trend indicators, icons, and labels to clearly communicate changes 
and meaning at a glance. The component is locale-aware, enabling proper formatting for numbers, dates, and currencies 
across regions. It also offers customizable colors and color variants, making it easy to match different statuses, 
themes, or branding requirements.
