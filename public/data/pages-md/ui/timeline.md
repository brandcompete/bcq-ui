The <code>timeline</code> component visually organizes events in chronological order, helping users quickly understand 
progression over time. It can display milestones, statuses, or activities using vertical or horizontal layouts 
with clear markers and connectors. Ideal for histories, workflows, or roadmaps, it improves readability 
and adds structure to sequential information. If you are looking for a real-life integration of the component, 
check our <a href="/getting-started/roadmap" v-link>roadmap</a> page.
