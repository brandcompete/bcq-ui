A versatile component that supports both mutually exclusive and multi-select interaction patterns. 
It enables grouping related actions into a cohesive control, with clear visual feedback for active and inactive states. 
The behavior can be configured to enforce single selection or allow multiple simultaneous selections, depending on context. 
Designed for flexibility, it integrates seamlessly into toolbars and control panels where grouped actions are required. 
Built on top of the <a href="/ui/btn" v-link>btn</a> component.
