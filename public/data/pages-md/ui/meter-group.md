Our <code>meter-group</code> <a href="/ui" v-link>component</a> displays scalar measurements within a known range. The
<code>meter-group-item</code> sub-components support <code>@item-click</code>, <code>label</code>, <code>icon</code>, <code>color</code> array 
(using more than one color value will create a gradient) and much more.
