A special renderless component that generates <a href="/ui/color-picker" v-link>color</a> schemes of 10 (or more) <a href="/content/colors" v-link>colors</a> with lightness modifiers 
from a provided <code>hex</code> and <code>swatches</code> props, that can be used to brand applications build with 
BCQ and give them unique custom or corporate look and feel. Internally, <code>brand-color</code> is using our 
<a href="/content/reactive-style" v-link>reactive-style</a> component.
