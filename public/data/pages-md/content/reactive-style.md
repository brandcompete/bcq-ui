Our <code>reactive-style</code> component is an enhanced <code>style</code> tag that can be used directly in Vue 
templates allowing any rule, property or value to be reactive and respond to <code>data</code>, <code>prop</code> or 
<code>v-model</code> changes. Internally, <code>reactive-style</code> is being used in our 
<a href="/content/brand-color" v-link>brand-color</a> component.
