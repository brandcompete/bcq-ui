Our grid system is based on 12-column layout by 4 breakpoints, each one with another 12 columns. 
This creates visual consistency between layouts, while allowing flexibility across a wide variety of designs. 

To create tables or less complex layouts we recommend that you use our <a href="/content/table-layout" v-link>table-layout</a> 
component. If you are looking for ready-to-use full-featured grids, you should check our <a href="/ui/data-table" v-link>data-table</a>.
