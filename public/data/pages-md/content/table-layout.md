Our <code>table-layout</code> component, along with its <code>row</code> and <code>cell</code> sub-components renders 
tables with a few handy props, making use of the <a href="/content/utility-css-classes" v-link>utility classes</a> 
and several table-only related CSS classes (<code>.table-layout-tr</code> and <code>.table-layout-thead</code>) and is 
suitable for simple layouts or simple grids. If you need a full-featured grid solution, have a look at the 
<a href="/ui/data-table" v-link>data-table</a> and if you are building complex responsive layouts, you might want to check our 
<a href="/content/grid" v-link>grid</a> system.
