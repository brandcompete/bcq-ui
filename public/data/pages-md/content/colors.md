Colors play a crucial role in an application's usability and overall user experience. They help communicate hierarchy, 
guide user attention, and make interfaces easier to understand at a glance. Consistent color usage also reinforces 
brand identity and creates a cohesive visual language across the product. When used thoughtfully, colors can improve 
accessibility, readability, and the emotional connection users have with the application.

BCQ supports two types of color strategy - colors with variants and colors with modifies, both of which are explained on this page.

and <a href="/ui/color-picker" v-link>color-picker</a> components, which can help you bran your application consistently.
